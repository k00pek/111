# Opening scenes

One scene for each chunk that may open a lesson. 157 scenes. The nuclear chunk is in bold.

Five chunks wait until the talk is already going: in the end, so far, for good, by now, on one's own.

The next scene changes the pronoun and the time when the chunk allows it.

---

## 1. have to

Scene: You and a friend at home. He asks about you, now.
Other: Do you **have to** go?
Cue: Он спрашивает про тебя, про сейчас. Скажи, что надо идти.
Phrase: I **have to** go.
