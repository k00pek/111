# Chunk build

One pattern and one verb form for each of the 162 chunks in the nuclear list.
This file does not contain the finished sentences. The bot does not read this file.

Patterns:

- ready. The chunk is already the reply, or the subject is already inside it.
- subject. Put a subject in front of the chunk.
- be. Put a subject and a form of be in front of the chunk.
- question. The sentence is a question. The chunk starts it, or the question is built around the chunk.

The subject, when the pattern needs one, is one of: I, you, we, they, he, she, it. A person's name works as he or she. It is for a thing. One chunk still makes one sentence, so the sentence uses one subject.

The form is one of the eight B1 forms when the chunk takes one. A modal, an imperative, or a chunk with no verb keeps its own label: used to, had better, might as well, would like, would, can, could, imperative, no verb. Past Continuous and Past Perfect Simple are among the eight, and no chunk here takes either one as its form.
