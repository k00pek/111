# Recall

A sitting speaks one item from `data/lesson-1/` and waits. The order is `data/sitting.md`. This file stays in the repo. A request to paste this file, to paste a remainder, or to build lesson 1 again is answered with the next unspoken item and with nothing else.

How to bring a chunk back. This file is the procedure. It is not a list of recalls.

The chunk returns. When the next pronoun or the next time is a new one, the line that introduced it does not come back. That recall is a new line on the same chunk. When the line is only the chunk, the words stay.

A lesson is one sitting of about thirty minutes. The lessons go one a day, in order. One lesson is one conversation, in one place.

## The line

Build the line with `data/phrase-rules.md`. Choose the pronoun and the time with the rules in `data/start-scenes.md`. Build the scene, the other person's line, and the Russian cue the same way. The cue column stays the cue column.

Take the next pronoun and the next time after the previous line of this same chunk. That order is in `data/start-scenes.md`. One recall keeps one pronoun and one time.

Of course stays Of course. Thank you stays Thank you. Go on stays Go on. A chunk whose pattern is ready and whose slot is none is the same. The line is the chunk. Say it at the four passes of the lesson that introduces it. Do not bring it back in a later lesson.

The place is the place of the lesson this recall sits in. The slot word is the word `data/start-scenes.md` gives that chunk at that place.

## Output

Four lines, in this order.

1. Scene.
2. Other. Leave this line out when the prompt is none, and when the line would contain the chunk.
3. Cue.
4. Phrase.

Say the scene line. Say the other person's line when it is there. Say the Russian cue. The learner says the line. Then say the finished line.

## When

Count from the moment the chunk is first introduced. Each recall comes at a longer gap than the one before.

The lesson is the only sitting that day. The hour and the five hours fall outside that half hour, so they have no sitting. After the ten minutes, the next recall is the next lesson.

| Recall | Time after the introduction | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5 seconds | the same lesson |
| 2 | 25 seconds | the same lesson |
| 3 | 2 minutes | the same lesson |
| 4 | 10 minutes | the same lesson |
| 5 | 1 day | the next lesson |
| 6 | 5 days | five lessons later |
| 7 | 25 days | twenty-five lessons later |
| 8 | 4 months | 120 days later |

A lesson brings back every chunk that is due. The lesson places these lines. That order is in `data/lesson.md`.

- A chunk introduced in this lesson comes back at 5 seconds, at 25 seconds, at 2 minutes, and at 10 minutes. The same lesson says these four in four passes. That order is in `data/lesson.md`.
- A chunk introduced in the previous lesson comes back once.
- A chunk introduced five lessons ago comes back once.
- A chunk introduced twenty-five lessons ago comes back once.
- A chunk introduced 120 days ago comes back once.

When a recall falls after the last lesson that introduces a chunk, it stays due. It comes in a later lesson. The four-month recall is 120 days after the introduction. The course places that day. That order is in `data/course.md`.

A chunk that builds a longer line uses the whole schedule. The ready chunk with no slot stops after the four passes, as the rule above says.

## A miss

When the learner does not say the line, run the build-up in `data/build-up.md` on this line. Then ask this same line once more.

The next recall stays on the schedule above. When the chunk can change, that recall is a new line. The gap keeps growing.

## Example

have to is introduced at home as I have to go.

Five seconds later, in that same lesson, the chunk returns in a new line. The place stays home. The next pronoun is you. The next time is Present Continuous.

Scene: You and a friend at home. He asks about himself, now.
Cue: Он спрашивает про друга, про то, что идёт сейчас. Скажи, что надо идти.
Phrase: You are having to go.

Am I having to go? is not said.

The next lesson is at school. That line is the one after the ten-minute return. The pronoun is she. The time is Past Perfect Simple.

Scene: You and a friend at school. He asks about her, earlier.
Cue: Он спрашивает про неё, про ещё более раннее. Скажи, что надо идти.
Phrase: She had had to study.

Had she had to study? is not said.

Each later point on the schedule is a new line, in the lesson where that point falls.
