# Start scenes

How to build one opening scene for a lesson. One scene introduces one phrase. This file is the procedure. It is not a list of scenes.

Build the learner's phrase with `data/phrase-rules.md`. Choose the pronoun and the time first. The form on the chunk's row is its usual time. The scene uses another of the eight forms when the rules below allow it.

The next scene uses a different pronoun and a different time. One scene keeps one chunk, one pronoun, and one time.

Bringing a phrase back later, and building a long phrase from its end, belong to the rest of the lesson. This file stops at the opening.

## Output

Four lines, in this order.

1. Scene. Who is there, where, and why someone speaks.
2. Other. One short B1 line from the other person. The learner hears it and does not produce it. When the prompt is none, leave this line out.
3. Cue. Russian. The person, the time, and the cue column from `data/nuclear-list.md`.
4. Phrase. The learner's one sentence, in the chosen pronoun and time.

## Scene

Two people: the learner and a friend. he or she adds one third person. they is a group, or people who are not here. it is a thing that is already in the scene.

One place: home, shop, station, work, phone.

The slot takes the word for that place. It takes that word once.

| Place | Thing | Verb | News |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| home | key | go | The dog is here |
| shop | bread | pay | The shop is closed |
| station | ticket | wait | The train is late |
| work | letter | work | The letter is ready |
| phone | battery | call | The phone is off |

bread, milk, and time take no a. Every other thing takes a when it is new, and the when it is the one already in the scene.

- thing, of + thing, and a thing slot marked before: the thing for the place.
- verb: the verb for the place, in the base form. At home the verb is go: She has to go. keep on takes -ing: She keeps on going. get to do replaces do, in the phrase and in the question: She got to go. Did she get to go?
- thing or verb: the thing. feel like takes the place verb with -ing: feels like going. get used to takes it: She is getting used to it. good at takes sport: She is good at sport. afford to takes can and the place verb: She can afford to go. Can she afford to go?
- place or verb: home is at home, shop is at the shop, station is at the station, work is at work, phone is on the phone. She ended up at home.
- place: home stays home, work stays work, and shop, station, and phone take the. She got home. She got to work. head to already has to: she is heading to the shop, she is heading home, she is heading to work. The question drops to before home as well. Did she get home? Is she heading home? go around uses the shop. She went around the shop. Did she go around the shop?
- to + place, and to + place or a thing: that place. home, here, and there take no to. She went back to the shop. I went back home. The phone is not a place you go back to. go back, come back, and get back use home, the shop, the station, or work.
- person, and with + person: you, the friend. he is him, she is her, they is them.
- thing or person: the thing for the place, for look for and pick up. She is looking for the key. She will pick up the ticket. look after, come up to, hold up, catch up, rely on, take care of, next to, and look like take a person: him when the pronoun is he, her when it is she, them when it is they, and you otherwise. She is looking after him. She looks like her. think about, believe in, fond of, deal with, take for granted, in front of, and heard of take it. She is thinking about it. They believe in it. She is fond of it. She will deal with it. She takes it for granted. She is in front of it. She has heard of it.
- none or thing: the thing for the place. take off at the station takes no thing, and the cue is «взлететь». At the other places the thing is coat, and the cue stays «снять это». turn up takes no thing. She turned up. Did she turn up?
- clause: the news sentence for the place. When it is not the start of the line, its first word loses the capital. In a past time, is becomes was. What if the dog is here? I didn't know the dog was here. When the chunk has a particle, the clause stays after the whole chunk. She has found out the dog is here. She pointed out the dog was here. turn out keeps it: It turned out the dog was here. I'm afraid is the refusal I'm afraid not. The news stays in the other person's line. make sure does not use the station: you do not make sure that the train is late. She made sure the letter was ready.
- none: nothing follows the chunk.
- beside: the place verb, in the scene time, and the chunk at the end. The question uses that same verb. Is she going at the moment? Does she go all the time? Will she go in advance?

These chunks do not take the thing in the place table.

run out of keeps the chosen place and uses milk at home, bread at the shop, time at the station, time at work, and battery on the phone. battery takes no the. You have run out of battery.

turn into takes a, because the result is new. She turned into a problem. come up with takes a: She came up with a plan. go for takes a: She is going for a walk. it, music, and sport take nothing: She got away with it. She took up music. She is good at sport. advice takes the: She acted on the advice. The other words in that table take the.

bear in mind puts it before the particle. She will bear it in mind. put together puts the noun between the verb and together, in the phrase and in the question. She put the letter together. Did she put the letter together? in mind takes the job. She has the job in mind.

in advance uses the phone, so the verb is call. She will call in advance. Will she call in advance?

go into takes it. She will go into it. Will she go into it?

happen to takes be and the place, in the Present Simple or the Past Simple. She happened to be at home. Did she happen to be at home? She happens to be on the phone.

in common keeps we. We have music in common. Do we have music in common?

A person slot uses the word in the person bullet. I and we use you.

## Pronoun

Choose one: I, you, we, they, he, she, it.

- I is the learner.
- you is the friend.
- we is the learner and the friend.
- he or she is the third person. A name works as he or she.
- they is a group.
- it is a thing.

Patterns subject, be, have, beside, and question with have take this pronoun.

it is the subject only when a thing can do that or be that: about to, have to, used to, ought to, better off, would like, turn up, turn into, come back, go back, get out, come out, get away, go for, get up, end up, run out of, take off, happen to, could hardly, in front of, next to, on the way, to blame, in the way, up to date. Every other chunk takes a person. She is supposed to pay. She put the letter together. She is good at sport. She will find out the dog is here.

bound to keeps it, and the verb is work. It is bound to work. It was bound to work.

A chunk that already contains its pronoun keeps that pronoun. I don't know, I think, I don't think, I thought, and I'll tell you what keep I. What do you think?, Do you know?, What do you mean?, and What are you doing? keep you. They say keeps they. It takes, that's it, up to you, take place, no good, to do with, make sense, and turn out keep it. The you inside up to you stays you. It is up to you. It makes sense. in common keeps we. There is / there are keeps there.

When a chunk cannot change its pronoun, the next scene changes the chunk so the pronoun still changes.

In the other person's line, I and you change places. The learner's I is the friend's you. The learner's you is the friend's I. we, they, he, she, and it stay the same in both lines.

## Time

Choose one of the eight forms in `data/b1-tenses.md`.

The other person's line and the learner's phrase use this same time. The cue names it in Russian, before the learner hears the phrase.

- Present Simple: про сейчас. Scene word: now
- Present Continuous: про то, что идёт сейчас. Scene word: now
- Present Perfect Simple: про то, что уже есть. Scene word: already
- Past Simple: про вчера. Scene word: yesterday
- Past Continuous: про тот момент. Scene word: at that moment
- Past Perfect Simple: про ещё более раннее. Scene word: earlier
- will: про решение. Scene word: as a decision
- going to: про план. Scene word: as a plan

used to uses про раньше. Its scene word is before. Any other row that is not one of the eight uses про сейчас. Its scene word is now.

The scene uses the form on the row. It uses another form only from the list for that pattern.

- subject or have, when the form column is one of the eight: any of the eight.
- be: Present Simple, Past Simple, or will. She is fond of it. She was fond of it. She will be fond of it.
- there is / there are: Present Simple, Past Simple, will, or going to. There is a key. There was a key. There will be a key.
- no sign of: Present Simple, Past Simple, Present Perfect Simple, Past Perfect Simple, will, or going to.
- beside: the time that chunk sets. at the moment and right now are Present Continuous. in advance is will. all the time is Present Simple.

Some chunks take fewer times than their pattern.

- meant to takes the Past Simple. I meant to wait.
- care for keeps the row: Present Simple, negative. She doesn't care for bread.
- look like takes the Present Simple or the Past Simple. She looks like her. She looked like her.
- take up does not take the Present Simple. She took up music.
- find out, keep on, turn up, go into, get to do, feel like, bother to, and turn out do not take a continuous form. She has found out the dog is here. She keeps on going. She turned up. She went into it. She got to go. She felt like going. She bothered to go. It turned out the dog was here.
- supposed to and bound to do not take will. She is supposed to pay. It is bound to work.
- happen to takes the Present Simple or the Past Simple.
- to do with takes the Present Simple or the Past Simple. It has to do with the key. It had to do with the key.

These keep the form on the row:

- a row marked no verb, such as of course and thank you
- a row marked imperative
- a modal label: used to, had better, might as well, would like, would, can, could, ought to

These chunks set the time. The scene uses that time.

- at the moment and right now take Present Continuous
- all the time takes Present Simple
- in advance takes will
- so far takes Present Perfect Simple
- in the end and for good take Past Simple
- by now takes Present Simple

When a chunk cannot change its time, the next scene changes the chunk so the time still changes.

## Scene line

You and a friend, then the place, then why someone speaks, then the scene word for the time.

| Place | In the line |
| --- | --- |
| home | at home |
| shop | at the shop |
| station | at the station |
| work | at work |
| phone | on the phone |

| Prompt | Why |
| --- | --- |
| question | He asks about the phrase's subject |
| invitation | He invites you. thank you: He gives you something |
| request | He asks you to help |
| news | He tells you the news |
| none | The learner speaks first |

The question names the subject: you, himself, the two of you, him, her, them, it. there uses He asks what is there.

You and a friend at home. He asks about her, yesterday.

## Prompt

The prompt is the last column of the chunk's row in `data/chunk-build.md`.

- question. The other person asks with the base form of the chunk and the slot word. The learner answers with the finished phrase. Do you have to go? She had to go. The friend's pronoun follows the rule above: Do you have to go? for the learner's I. Am I looking for the key? for the learner's You are looking for the key.
- invitation. The line does not contain the chunk. of course and all right use Are you coming? thank you uses Here you are.
- request. The line does not contain the chunk. Can you help? In the past, Could you help? With will, Will you help? With going to, Are you going to help?
- news. The line does not contain the chunk. It is the news sentence for the place. is in that sentence changes with the time: is, was, has been, had been, will be, or is going to be. Present Continuous uses is. Past Continuous uses was. The dog is here. The shop was closed. The train will be late.
- none. No line from the other person. The learner speaks first. when it comes to is already the sentence, so its prompt is none. When it comes to the key. A chunk that is already a question keeps the question mark. What do you think? Do you know the dog is here? What do you mean? What are you doing?

Question shapes. The first word of the chunk takes the ending. The rest of the chunk stays, then the slot word. looking for the key. found out the time.

there is / there are asks with there and be. Is there a key? Was there a key? Will there be a key? Is there going to be a key? no sign of asks Is there a sign of the key? Was there a sign of the key? Has there been a sign of the key? The thing follows of.

- Present Simple: Do or Does + subject + base form
- Past Simple: Did + subject + base form
- will: Will + subject + base form
- going to: a form of be + subject + going to + the slot verb. The chunk going to is that frame, so it is not repeated. Is she going to go?
- Present Continuous: a form of be + subject + the verb with -ing
- Present Perfect Simple: have or has + subject + the participle
- Past Continuous: was or were + subject + the verb with -ing
- Past Perfect Simple: had + subject + the participle

For pattern be, the question is the form of be, then the subject, then the chunk, then the slot word. Is she fond of the key? Was she fond of the key? will splits around the subject: Will she be fond of the key?

For pattern have, the question is Do or Does, then the subject, then have, then the chunk. Do you have any idea? A slot marked before stays before the chunk. Does she have the key in common? question with have uses that same order. Does she have the key in mind?

one's matches the subject in the question too. Has she made up her mind?

have got and have got to ask with have, not with do. Has she got the key? Has she got to go?

A modal keeps its own word. The modal comes first, then the subject, then the rest of the chunk, then the slot word. Ought she to go? Can she tell the dog is here? Would she like the key? Would she say the dog is here? Had she better go? Might she as well go? used to asks with did and use. Did she use to go? could hardly drops hardly in the question. Could she go? The learner says She could hardly go.

A chunk that already contains its subject uses that subject once. I don't know, I think, and I don't think drop I, and the question stays positive. Do you know the dog is here? The learner says I don't know the dog is here. I thought uses Did you think the dog was here? it takes uses Does it take time? they say uses Do they say the dog is here? up to you uses Is it up to you? take place uses Did it take place? no good uses Is it no good? to do with uses Does it have to do with the key?

## Cue

The first words come from the prompt.

- question: Он спрашивает
- invitation: Он зовёт. For thank you: Он даёт вещь.
- request: Он просит
- news: Он говорит
- none: no first words

Then the person and the time.

- I: про тебя
- you: про друга
- we: про вас двоих
- he: про него
- she: про неё
- they: про них
- it: про это

Then «Скажи, что» and the cue column. The cue column is already the line. The sense gloss is not used here. The English chunk stays out.

For prompt none, the cue is only «Скажи, что» and the cue column.

## What may open

A short ready reply may open a lesson: Of course, Thank you, I think so.

A sentence with one subject and one slot may open a lesson.

These wait until the conversation is already going: in the end, so far, for good, by now, on one's own.

## Examples

Scene: You and a friend at home. He invites you, now.
Other: Are you coming?
Cue: Он зовёт про тебя, про сейчас. Скажи, что конечно.
Phrase: Of course.

Scene: You and a friend at home. He asks about her, yesterday.
Other: Did she have to go?
Cue: Он спрашивает про неё, про вчера. Скажи, что надо идти.
Phrase: She had to go.
